The divide

One division turns depth into size: farther points land closer to the middle. That is the entire camera.

The setup in your code

shapes/vec3.go puts the camera at the origin staring down +Z (X right, Y down-screen, like the terminal). Projection is one line per axis:

px = cx + x·focal/z      py = cy + y·focal/z

Take your cube's numbers: a vertex at x=10, z=32, focal=48 (your pixel height on an 80×24 terminal). Offset = 10·48/32 = 15 px right of center. Now double the depth to 64: offset = 10·48/64 = 7.5 px. Twice as far, half as far from the middle — perspective is just that ratio (Wikipedia).

See it

Drag the focal slider below — but first predict: halving focal from 48 to 24 makes the cube…? Then check the V7 readout: it shows one vertex's full journey from (x, y, z) to pixel on every frame.

Focal is zoom. Bigger focal = bigger and flatter (faraway objects shrink less). Your main.go sets focal to the pixel height every frame, which is why the cube keeps its size on resize. And z ≤ 0 returns false — your cube behind the camera projects nothing test — because dividing by zero or negative depth is meaningless, so the edge is skipped.
